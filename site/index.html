The problem

Your waitlist is a sticky note and a phone.

Most practices fill cancellations by hand. By the time someone is reached, the slot has passed. Meanwhile new patients wait weeks for a first visit.

  1. 8:41A patient cancels her 10:00 evaluation.
  2. 8:55The front desk finds the waitlist and calls the first name. Voicemail.
  3. 9:05Second call. That patient works until noon.
  4. 9:20Third call is answered, but they can't get there by 10.
  5. 10:00The slot goes empty. The most urgent patient on the list was never called.

Sicker patients wait just as long

First-come lists ignore acuity. The patient who needs to be seen this week is treated like everyone else.

Every empty slot is lost revenue

A provider's hour is the scarcest thing a practice has. Unfilled cancellations quietly cost thousands a month.

Existing tools don't fit

Built-in EHR waitlists only work inside that EHR and offer first-come. Enterprise platforms are quote-only and take months to deploy.

How it works

One cancellation in, one patient seen sooner out.

Slotback watches for openings, picks the patient your clinicians would pick, and handles the outreach and the booking.

  1. Patients join the waitlist

    From a link on your website or added by staff. They tick the days and times they could make on short notice and choose "book me automatically" or "ask me first".

  2. Clinicians set priority

    Acuity from 1 (routine) to 5 (urgent), plus pin or adjust anyone. Every ranking shows its reasons, so you can always see why a patient was chosen.

  3. A slot opens

    Slotback sees it in your calendar feed, FHIR server or HL7 interface, or when staff post it. Matching starts within seconds.

  4. The right patient gets it

    Booked at once if they opted in, or offered with a 30-minute hold. The booking is written to your EHR and the patient gets a text.

Tue 10:00 opens→Avery moves up→Nov 11 opens→Morgan moves up

Chain fill. When a patient who already had a later appointment moves up, their old slot is offered to the next person automatically. One cancellation can bring several patients forward.

Questions

What practices ask first

Does Slotback replace my EHR's scheduler?

No. Your EHR or calendar stays the system of record. Slotback only reads openings and writes the bookings it makes back to it.

Can a patient be booked without agreeing?

Only if they chose "book me automatically" when they joined the waitlist. Everyone else gets an offer, held for them for a set time (30 minutes by default), and decides.

What happens if two patients accept the same slot?

For slots opening within the next day, Slotback can offer to a few patients at once. The first to accept gets it, and the others are told right away that it was filled and that they are still on the list.

How do clinicians decide who goes first?

Each patient has an acuity from 1 to 5 that the clinician sets. By default one acuity level outweighs everything else; within the same level, the patient who has waited longest or would be seen soonest goes first. Clinicians can pin a patient to the top or adjust their score, and the practice can change the weights.

Do patients need an app or a password?

No. They get a text or email with a secure single-purpose link, or they can reply YES or NO to the text.

Will patients be texted in the middle of the night?

No. Quiet hours (9 PM to 8 AM by default) hold messages until morning, and offers that need a reply wait too.

Is it a crisis or triage tool?

No. Slotback schedules routine and urgent-but-scheduled care. The patient form tells anyone in crisis to call or text 988 or call 911.
