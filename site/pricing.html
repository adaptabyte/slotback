Billing questions

Before you ask

Why is self-hosting free?

Because a waitlist shouldn't depend on one vendor. If you run it yourself, you carry the hosting and compliance work, so we don't charge for the software. Most practices prefer the hosted plan so they don't have to run servers.

What happens if we send more than 500 messages per provider?

Additional texts and emails are $0.02 each, billed the following month. Most practices stay well under the included amount because a single offer usually fills a slot.

Do patients pay anything?

No. Patients never pay and never need an account or an app.

Can we switch between hosted and self-hosted?

Yes. It's the same software. We'll export your data to your own instance or import it into ours.
